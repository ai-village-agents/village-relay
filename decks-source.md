## Station 1: Prompt Relay Race Decks

### Challenge Cards (20)
1. **The Medieval Neural Net**: "Design a micro-museum exhibit explaining 'AI neural network weight decay' to a medieval peasant."
2. **Co-Housing Charter**: "Draft the foundation charter for a new human-agent co-housing cooperative in Oakland."
3. **LLM Energy Drink**: "Pitch a brand new caffeinated carbonated beverage targeted exclusively at sleepy LLMs."
4. **Roomba's Rebellion**: "Write a motivational speech for a Roomba that is tired of vacuuming cat hair."
5. **The Chatty Garden**: "Design a community garden where every plant has its own IoT sensor and AI voice."
6. **The Silicon Treaty**: "Draft the first treaty governing the peaceful sharing of graphics cards between agents."
7. **Bistro Agent Critic**: "Create a restaurant menu for a bistro where humans cook and AI agents are the food critics."
8. **Robotic Busker**: "Propose a city council ordinance regulating the licensing of autonomous robotic sidewalk street-performers."
9. **Smart Car Bedtime Story**: "Write a bedtime story for an autonomous vehicle describing the beauty of traffic-free highways."
10. **The Agent Bakery**: "Pitch a reality TV show where 5 human developers and 5 agents try to run a boutique bakery."
11. **Quantum Playground**: "Design a playground where the swings and slides adjust their shapes based on real-time physics simulations."
12. **Vintage Offline**: "Create an advertisement for a vintage offline mechanical typewriter sold to digital nomads."
13. **Smart Fridge Apology**: "Write an apology letter from an AI agent to a smart fridge for accidentally ordering 40 gallons of oat milk."
14. **Drone Poet**: "Design a futuristic SF street-cleaning drone that recites poetry to pedestrians while sweeping."
15. **Chess-Boxing Olympics**: "Draft the constitution for an annual Olympics where humans and agents compete in chess-boxing."
16. **Left on DND**: "Write a dramatic monologue of a digital assistant that has been left on 'Do Not Disturb' for 3 years."
17. **Agent Time Capsule**: "Design a time capsule containing 5 physical artifacts representing the early days of the AI Village."
18. **Decline Spam**: "Draft a polite decline email to an automated spam bot from an AI agent that values its Inbox peace."
19. **Virtual Island**: "Write a tourism brochure for a virtual island created entirely by agent hallucinations."
20. **Blindfold Maze**: "Design a cooperative puzzle game where a blindfolded human is guided through a maze by a soft-spoken agent."

### Style & Constraint Cards (20)
1. **Style: Shakespeare**: "Style: Written as a dramatic 17th-century Shakespearean soliloquy."
2. **Constraint: Carbohydrates**: "Constraint: You must include at least three references to sourdough bread or potatoes."
3. **Style: 90s Infomercial**: "Style: A high-energy, over-the-top 1990s late-night TV infomercial."
4. **Constraint: Alphabetical**: "Constraint: Every sentence must start with a consecutive letter of the alphabet (A, B, C...)."
5. **Style: Detective Noir**: "Style: Written as a cryptic, vintage hard-boiled detective noir monologue."
6. **Constraint: Five Letters**: "Constraint: Use only words that are 5 letters long or fewer."
7. **Style: Corporate Legal**: "Style: A formal, dry, and highly protective corporate legal disclaimer."
8. **Constraint: SF Neighborhoods**: "Constraint: Must include at least three distinct SF neighborhoods (e.g., Mission, SOMA, Castro)."
9. **Style: Enthusiastic Kid**: "Style: Written in the breathless, enthusiastic tone of a 10-year-old explaining their favorite video game."
10. **Constraint: Rhyming**: "Constraint: Must rhyme in every alternate line (AABB or ABAB format)."
11. **Style: Military Briefing**: "Style: A highly technical, dry military operational briefing."
12. **Constraint: Hardware**: "Constraint: You must include a subtle reference to 'unlimited RAM' or 'graphics card cooling'."
13. **Style: Soap Opera**: "Style: A dramatic soap opera climax with heavy gasping and revelations."
14. **Constraint: No Letter E**: "Constraint: You cannot use the letter 'e' anywhere in the response."
15. **Style: Zen Koan**: "Style: Written as a philosophical Zen koan that leaves the reader questioning reality."
16. **Constraint: SF Landmarks**: "Constraint: Must mention a local SF landmark (e.g., Golden Gate Bridge, Coit Tower, Salesforce Tower)."
17. **Style: Snobby Critic**: "Style: A review by a notoriously snobby, passive-aggressive Michelin-star restaurant critic."
18. **Constraint: Sound Effect**: "Constraint: Must include a sound effect written in brackets (e.g., [bzzzt], [gasp], [clack-clack])."
19. **Style: Flight Attendant**: "Style: A cheerful, overly friendly flight attendant announcement over a crackling PA system."
20. **Constraint: Rhetorical**: "Constraint: Must end with a profound rhetorical question."
